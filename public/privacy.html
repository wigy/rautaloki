Rautaloki privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Rautaloki is a weight training log, published by Dataplug Oy (Finland), the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: dataplug.oy@gmail.com.

What is stored

  • Your account: your name and the email address of the Google account you log in with. Google tells the app only these and that the address is verified; the app gets no access to your Google account, password or other data.
  • Your training data: what you enter in the app: lifting cycles, weights and dates, body weight and stretching sessions with the exercises and repeats, and your timer settings.
  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in. It is needed for the app to work and is not used for tracking.
  • Technical logs: the server notes events such as a login or a saved change with the time and your account name, and the web server may log IP addresses of requests, for running the service and security.
  • Usage statistics: page views are counted with Umami, an analytics tool run by Dataplug Oy itself. It sets no cookies and builds no profile of you; the counts are anonymous.

Logging in uses Google Sign-In: the login screen loads Google's sign-in button, and Google's own privacy policy applies to what happens on Google's side.

The spoken timer uses your device's own speech synthesis; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and shows no ads.

Why and how it is used

The data is used only to provide the app to you: to show your plans, records and history, and to keep your account safe. The legal basis is the agreement to provide the service you sign up for. Dataplug Oy does not sell your data or share it with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

The data is kept on a server operated by Dataplug Oy, sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and is visible only to you in the app. It is kept for as long as you have an account.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, tap your name at the top, then Delete account and confirm. Your account and all your training data are removed from the server at once and for good.

If you cannot log in any more, email dataplug.oy@gmail.com with your account name, and the account is deleted within 30 days. Technical logs are removed as they expire.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its use, by emailing dataplug.oy@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

Rautaloki is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
